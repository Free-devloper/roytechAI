# Frequently asked questions

## Can you take an idea from zero to launch?

Yes. RoyTech AI can start from a product idea, rough brief, existing workflow, Figma file, or a product that needs to be rebuilt. First, the team clarifies the smallest useful release.

## Do you build AI agents and RAG applications?

Yes — when they improve the real workflow. The studio designs the product experience, retrieval, guardrails, evaluation approach, integrations, and operational model around the feature. Agents are not added for novelty.

## Can you work with our in-house team?

Yes. RoyTech AI can extend the team, own a defined product area, modernize part of the platform, or work as a focused delivery squad alongside in-house people.

## Will we own the code and product?

That is the intended delivery model. The client team should be able to understand, operate, and continue the work after launch. Handover and documentation are planned from the start.

## How fast can an MVP ship?

The founder guide on the blog describes a three-week useful-release pattern: week 1 framing and system mapping, week 2 focused build sprints, week 3 launch and handover. Timeline still depends on scope. The estimator lists 3–12 weeks by base product type.

## Do you do n8n and CRM automation?

Yes. Automation and integrations is a core service: CRM, forms, payments, documents, HubSpot, Salesforce, webhooks, and n8n workflows. Use n8n when the process is event-driven and tool-to-tool. Use custom agents when the work needs reasoning, retrieval, or multi-step decisions.

## How do we start?

Share name, email, and what you are trying to make work. The site contact form and RoytechAI Assistant both send a build brief to the studio workflow. A human follows up. There is no public phone or generic inbox published on the marketing site.
